PROJECT: Lets be Friends!

A fun, creepy game about how companies collect our data and use it to build digital versions of us.

You face your own Digital Replica — an AI that watches your face, movements and reactions. The more it learns, the more unsettling it becomes. 😈

CURRENT PROGRESS 🚧
• Live camera + face tracking
• 478-point facial landmark tracking
• Smile & movement detection
• Hand tracking + automatic wave detection
• Digital Replica system
• Retro 1990s computer interface

FUTURE 🔮
More reactions, glitches, sound effects, challenges, data-themed gameplay and a final reveal showing just how much of "you" companies can build from your data.

THE GOAL:
Make you laugh at first...

...then make you wonder how much of your digital self is already out there. 👁️
