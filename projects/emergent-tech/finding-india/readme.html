01 / PROJECT

🌊 Finding India

A fun, chaotic Columbus exploration game where you cruise a ridiculously zoomed-in world map, trying to figure out where you are while surviving the dangers of the sea.


Your mission is simple:


Find India → Get the spices → Make it back to Europe.


Along the way, expect storms, navigation disasters, weird encounters, unexpected dangers, and hilarious ways to completely mess up your voyage.


🧭 Explore. Navigate. Survive. Get the spices. Don't get lost.
